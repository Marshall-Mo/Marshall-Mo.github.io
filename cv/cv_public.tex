\sect{EMPLOYMENT}
\entry{\textbf{Economist}, Amazon --- People eXperience and Technology (PXT) --- Central Science}{2025--present}

\vspace{0.3em}
Core member of the team building Amazon's platform for \textit{\textbf{Agentic Causal Evaluation}} of workforce programs at scale. Work spans the econometric infrastructure (experiments, matching, synthetic control, synthetic difference-in-differences) and an agentic layer on top of it: AI systems that automate parts of the scientific workflow itself, from research design to report drafting to code review.

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{itemize}
\small
  \item \textbf{Causal research-design agent} --- proposes candidate identification strategies, tests their assumptions in deterministic code before any estimate exists, and returns an auditable menu for an economist to approve.
  \item \textbf{Team- and organization-level evaluation} --- extended the platform from sites to teams and organizations (cluster synthetic DiD over time-varying team membership) so that AI-native ways of working can be designed, randomized and evaluated at the manager grain.
  \item \textbf{Methodology} --- aggregation of period-specific matching estimates with valid inference under serial correlation; surrogate-index estimation of long-run effects from short-run outcomes (linear and flexible-reweighting estimators with delta-method and bootstrap inference).
  \item \textbf{Scientific-writing agents} --- multi-agent systems that draft review-ready science documents from an approved design and raw analysis materials, with a retrieval-augmented writing assistant.
\end{itemize}

\entry{\textit{The Persona as a Regression on Its Data: What a Fine-Tuned Simulated Subject Can and Cannot Recover} \textnormal{(in progress)}}{}
\begin{itemize}
\small

\end{itemize}
